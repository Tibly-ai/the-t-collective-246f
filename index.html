The Standard of Purity

How we honor the leaf at every stage.

01

Sustainably Harvested

We source exclusively from family estates that reject synthetic pesticides and fertilizers. By prioritizing bio-diverse soil cultivation, we protect the natural ecosystems of the tea growing regions.

Pure Soil Chemistry
02

True Artisanal Craft

Our leaves are entirely hand-plucked and traditional-processed by master teamakers. Each step is deliberately managed to retain the complex botanical character of the local terroir.

Time-Honored Craft
03

Intentional Accessories

Every cup, teapot, and tea scoop is thoughtfully designed with minimalist utility in mind. Our wares are handcrafted in limited batches from high-fired stoneware and heat-tempered borosilicate glass.

Functional Artistry
